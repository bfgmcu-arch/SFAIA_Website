About Us

Students supporting local small businesses.

Student Led Business Solutions is a student-run organization helping small businesses use private, locally-run AI and budgeting tools at no cost.

Who We Are

Neighbors helping neighbors.

Student Led Business Solutions is a student-led nonprofit focused on putting useful business software directly into the hands of local small business owners.

Small shops rarely have an IT department or extra room in their budget for pricey software subscriptions. By building and installing locally-run tools for free, we help local owners save time and plan ahead.

Our Approach

Free of Charge: Our software and in-person installation visits are always free for local small businesses.

Local and Private: Everything runs on your own business computer so your financial records stay with you.

Community Focus: Students get hands-on experience while giving back to local businesses.

What Matters to Us

Our Core Principles

No Cost Barriers

Good planning tools should not belong only to big chains. We remove software fees and setup headaches so any local shop can benefit.

Full Data Privacy

Your financial records are your business. Every tool we install runs locally on your computer instead of sending data to outside cloud servers.

In-Person Help

We do not just email a download link. Our students visit your business, set up the software properly, and walk you through how to use it.
