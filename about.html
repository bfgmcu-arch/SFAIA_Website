About Our Organization

Students serving New Hyde Park’s independent businesses.

Student Led Business Solutions is a grassroots, student-run initiative committed to making practical AI and business planning tools accessible to neighborhood shops at no cost.

Who We Are

Neighbors helping neighbors thrive.

Student Led Business Solutions is based right here in New Hyde Park, NY. We bring together motivated students with a shared purpose: putting modern technology directly into the hands of local small business owners.

Small businesses are the backbone of our community, yet they rarely have dedicated IT teams or the budget for expensive enterprise software. By building and installing locally-run tools for free, we help local owners save time, plan ahead, and stay competitive.

Our Guiding Commitment

No Fees, Ever: Our tools and on-site installation visits are provided completely free to qualifying local businesses.

Local & Private: We install software directly on your own computer so sensitive financial records stay under your roof.

Community Education: Students gain meaningful, real-world engineering and service experience while supporting the town they live in.

Core Values

What Guides Our Work

01 · Access

Technology Without Barriers

Financial planning and automation tools shouldn’t belong exclusively to large corporations. We remove cost and technical complexity so any local shop can benefit.

02 · Trust

Complete Data Ownership

Small business finances are personal and sensitive. Every solution we offer is designed to run locally on your machine—never relying on external cloud data harvesting.

03 · Service

In-Person Partnership

We don’t just hand over a download link. Our students show up in person, configure the software properly, and make sure you feel confident using it.
