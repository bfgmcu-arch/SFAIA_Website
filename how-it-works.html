How It Works

A straightforward, four-step onboarding experience designed around busy small business owners—no technical background required.

Four Simple Steps

From First Message to Ready-to-Use Software

  1. 1

    Business Contacts Our Organization

    Reach out to Student Led Business Solutions by email or through our contact page to express your interest in receiving free AI budgeting and business software assistance.

    No obligation · 100% free
  2. 2

    Appointment Is Scheduled

    A member of our student team will follow up promptly to answer any initial questions and coordinate a convenient time to visit your business location in New Hyde Park.

    Flexible scheduling around your hours
  3. 3

    Students Install the Software Locally

    Our student volunteers arrive at your shop, install our local AI software directly onto your business computer, and verify that everything is configured securely and running smoothly.

    Runs locally · Zero cloud uploads
  4. 4

    Hands-On Demonstration Is Provided

    Once installed, we walk you through the software step by step—showing you how to manage budgets, review profit projections, and get the most value from your new tool.

    Clear, jargon-free training
