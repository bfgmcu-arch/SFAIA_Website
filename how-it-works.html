How It Works

Our simple 4-step process to getting started.

The Process

  1. Business Contacts Organization

    Reach out to us to express your interest in receiving free AI assistance for your small business.

  2. Appointment is Scheduled

    We will get back to you to set up a convenient time to meet at your business location.

  3. Students Install the Software

    Our student team will arrive at your location and install the local AI software on your computers.

  4. Demonstration is Provided

    After installation, we will provide a complete demonstration of the software so you know exactly how to use it.
