How It Works

Getting started in four simple steps.

You do not need any technical background. Our students handle the installation and walk you through everything in person.

The Process

From First Message to Ready-to-Use Software

  1. 1

    Reach Out to Our Team

    Send us an email or use our contact page to let us know you are interested in free AI budgeting assistance for your business.

    No cost or obligation
  2. 2

    Schedule a Visit

    We will follow up to answer any questions and pick a convenient time to meet at your business.

    Scheduled around your hours
  3. 3

    Students Install the Software

    Our student team arrives at your business, installs the local AI software on your computer, and confirms everything runs properly.

    Runs locally with no cloud uploads
  4. 4

    In-Person Demonstration

    After installation, we walk through the software together so you know how to manage budgets, view profit projections, and use every feature.

    Hands-on walkthrough
