Our Mission

Bridging the technology gap for New Hyde Park small businesses.

Modern automation and AI tools are rapidly transforming how large corporations plan budgets and forecast revenue, yet local small businesses are often priced out or overwhelmed by complex cloud subscriptions.

Student Led Business Solutions was created to change that. As a student-led nonprofit initiative based in New Hyde Park, we develop and install locally-run software directly on your business computers—giving you modern capabilities at zero cost.
