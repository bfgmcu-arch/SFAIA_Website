Our Mission

Helping local small businesses use modern tools without the cost.

Larger companies have budgets for specialized software and analytics, while small local shops are often stuck with expensive subscriptions or complex cloud platforms.

Student Led Business Solutions is based in New Hyde Park and dedicated to helping small businesses access AI automation for free. We build and install locally-run tools directly on your business computers so you get useful technology without paying for it.

Why We Focus on Local Software

Many business tools require uploading your financial records to third-party cloud servers and paying a recurring monthly bill.

Because our tools run locally on your own computer, you keep full control over your numbers and never have to worry about subscription fees or outside data sharing.
