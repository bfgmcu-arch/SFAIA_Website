Our Services

Empowering your business with locally-run AI solutions.

AI Budgeting Tool

Our current main offering is a powerful AI budgeting tool designed specifically for small businesses. It operates completely free of charge.

This software helps you with:

  • Budgeting
  • Profit projections
  • Financial decisions

Local AI Software

We believe in privacy and security. That's why our AI tools run locally on your own computer. You don't have to worry about your sensitive financial data being sent to external servers or cloud services.

Installation Process

We handle the technical setup so you don't have to. Our students will come to your business, install the software on your computer, ensure it is configured properly, and make sure it runs correctly within your local environment.
