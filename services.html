How It Works

Private Software, Simple Setup

Local Business Software

We care about privacy and security, so all of our tools run locally on your own computer.

You do not have to worry about sensitive business records or customer information being sent to outside servers or cloud services.

Installation Process

We take care of the technical setup so you do not have to. Our students come to your business, install the software on your computer, and make sure it is configured properly.

Once everything is running smoothly, we walk you through how to use it step by step.
